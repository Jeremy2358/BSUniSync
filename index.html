About BSUniSync

BSUniSync is an academic analytics and decision-support portal designed to support student performance monitoring, teaching effectiveness evaluation, and early identification of academic concerns.

The current prototype uses sample academic and SETE records and a Decision Tree model trained on synthetic data. The model has not been validated against actual student outcomes.

Integration with institutional systems, authenticated faculty access, and permanent record storage are proposed future enhancements. The portal is not currently connected to university databases.

Use fictional or non-identifiable information only. Student records and predictions are retained during the current page session. Display name and theme preferences are stored locally when browser storage is available.
